FAQ

Questions?
We've got answers.

Everything you need to know about working with TB Yetter Plumbing. Can't find what you're looking for? Call us.

Do you offer emergency plumbing services?
Yes! We offer 7-day emergency service with same-day response. For urgent issues like burst pipes or major leaks, we typically arrive within 2 hours.
Are you licensed and insured?
Absolutely. We're fully licensed, bonded, and insured with $2M in liability coverage. A licensed plumber is on every job — never an unlicensed apprentice working alone.
How much do you charge?
We provide upfront, flat-rate pricing before any work begins. No hidden fees, no surprises. We also offer free estimates for larger projects like water heater replacements and repipes.
What areas do you serve?
We serve all of Salt Lake County including Salt Lake City, West Jordan, Sandy, Murray, Riverton, South Jordan, Draper, Midvale, Herriman, and West Valley City.
Do you warranty your work?
Yes! All our work comes with a satisfaction guarantee. If you're not happy, we'll make it right. Specific warranties vary by job type — ask for details when you get your estimate.
