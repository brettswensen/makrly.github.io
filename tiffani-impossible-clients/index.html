Scenario 1 of 3

The Job-Hopping Veteran

"I'll never qualify with my work history."

👤 The Client
A disabled veteran with a Certificate of Eligibility who can buy with zero down. He's had 6 jobs in the last 2 years and is 100% disabled. His spouse thinks homeownership is a pipe dream.
❌ The Problem
"I have no stability. Six jobs in two years? I'm unqualified. And I'm disabled — who would give me a loan?"
How Tiffani Solved It

The Job-Hopping Veteran

✅ The Strategy
Tiffani walked through his 2-year work history. Six jobs — but every single one was in the same industry. And there was never more than a 3-day gap between positions.

That showed consistency, not chaos. He qualified for a VA loan with his Certificate of Eligibility.
🎁 The Bonus Win
Because he's 100% disabled, Tiffani also got his property taxes removed entirely. His monthly payment dropped even further — more money in his pocket every month.
🦸‍♀️ Problem Solved
Scenario 2 of 3

The Contingent Buyer

"I can't buy without selling first."

👤 The Client
Owns a home in Herriman and wants to move to Cottonwood Heights — a more coveted, competitive area. Her agent told her contingent offers won't even be looked at.
❌ The Problem
"I have to sell before I can buy. But I can't buy without selling first. It's a chicken-and-egg problem in the most competitive market around. I'm stuck."
How Tiffani Solved It

The Contingent Buyer

✅ The Strategy
Her debt-to-income on a conventional loan was too tight — she'd need to sell first. But on an FHA loan, the DTI limits are more flexible.

Tiffani realized: she could qualify carrying BOTH mortgage payments at once. That meant buying Cottonwood Heights without a sale contingency.
🎁 The Bonus Win
She closed on the new house, had 2 full weeks to move, then sold the Herriman home. No bridge loan. No double move. No storage unit.

Her agent's "impossible" became a seamless transition from one home to the next.
🦸‍♀️ Problem Solved
Scenario 3 of 3

The Cash-Strapped Investor

"I have assets but no cash. This deal dies."

👤 The Client
Found a $400,000 investment property that's fairly priced with huge potential. Needs 25% down — about $100,000. Self-employed, so traditional income verification doesn't work.
❌ The Problem
"I own a property free and clear, but I need that equity to buy this new one. I'm self-employed — no traditional lender counts my income. Without $100K liquid, this deal dies."
How Tiffani Solved It

The Cash-Strapped Investor

✅ The Strategy
He owned an investment property free and clear. Tiffani structured a cash-out DSCR loan on that property.

DSCR = Debt Service Coverage Ratio. Based on the property's future rental income, not his personal income. Perfect for self-employed investors.
🎁 The Bonus Win
He pulled $100K from the paid-off property as his down payment. The new property had two entrances — rented to two families for ~$2,500 total, covering the new mortgage.

Zero personal income used. Zero cash out of pocket. One smart loan structure.
🦸‍♀️ Problem Solved
