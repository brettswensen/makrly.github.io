Three borrowers. Three "impossible" situations. And how they got solved.

Scenario 1 of 3

The Job-Hopping Veteran

"I'll never qualify with my work history."

The Client
Veteran with Certificate of Eligibility — can buy with zero down
6 jobs in 2 years
100% disabled
Spouse says homeownership is a pipe dream
The Problem
"I have no stability. Six jobs in two years? I'm unqualified. And I'm disabled — who would give me a loan?"
The Solution

The Job-Hopping Veteran

What Worked
Every job was in the same industry
Never more than a 3-day gap between positions — showed consistency, not chaos
Qualified for VA loan with COE
The Extra Win
100% disabled = property taxes removed entirely
Lower monthly payment, more money in pocket
Scenario 2 of 3

The Contingent Buyer

"I can't buy without selling first."

The Client
Owns in Herriman, wants Cottonwood Heights
Agent says contingent offers won't even be looked at
The Problem
"I have to sell before I can buy. But I can't buy without selling first. Chicken-and-egg in the most competitive market around."
The Solution

The Contingent Buyer

What Worked
Conventional DTI was too tight — needed to sell first
FHA has more flexible DTI limits
Could qualify carrying both mortgages at once
Bought Cottonwood Heights without a sale contingency
The Extra Win
Closed on new house, had 2 weeks to move, then sold Herriman home
No bridge loan. No double move.
Scenario 3 of 3

The Cash-Strapped Investor

"I have assets but no cash. This deal dies."

The Client
$400K investment property, fairly priced, huge potential
Needs 25% down — about $100K
Self-employed — traditional income verification doesn't work
The Problem
"I own a property free and clear but need that equity to buy. No traditional lender counts my income. Without $100K liquid, this deal dies."
The Solution

The Cash-Strapped Investor

What Worked
Owned investment property free and clear
Structured cash-out DSCR loan on that property
DSCR = property's future rental income, not personal income
Pulled $100K for down payment on new property
The Extra Win
New property had two entrances — rent to two families
~$2,500 total rent covers the new mortgage
Zero personal income used. Zero cash out of pocket.
The Pattern

What All Three Have in Common

Every client had been told "impossible" before
The veteran was ready to give up on homeownership
The buyer was told she'd never get into Cottonwood Heights
The investor thought his deal was dead
They weren't impossible. They just needed the right loan program.
VA loans. FHA flexibility. DSCR for investors.
Most lenders show one path. I find the one that works.
