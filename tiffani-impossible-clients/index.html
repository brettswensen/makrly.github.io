Scenario 1 of 3

The Job-Hopping Veteran

A volunteer "becomes" this person. Make it funny. Get into character.

🙋‍♂️

Volunteer: Introduce yourself to the group. Your name, your story, your situation. Make it real.

👤 You Are...
A veteran who served in the military. You have your Certificate of Eligibility and know you can buy with zero down. You're also 100% disabled.

You came to this meeting thinking: "I'll never qualify."
❌ Your "Impossible" Problem
"I have 6 jobs in 2 years. No lender will touch me. I'm disabled, I have no stability, and my spouse thinks buying a home is a pipe dream. Why would anyone give me a loan?"
🎤

Tiffani takes over now.
She'll ask questions, dig into your situation, and strategize a solution right here — live.

💬 Group Question
Is this actually impossible? What would you do?
The Reveal

The Job-Hopping Veteran — Solved

✅ What Tiffani Found
She walked through his 2-year work history. Six jobs, yes — but every single one was in the same industry. And there was never more than a 3-day gap between positions.

That showed consistency, not chaos. He qualified for a VA loan with his Certificate of Eligibility — 6 jobs and all.
🎁 The Bonus Win
Because he's 100% disabled, Tiffani also got his property taxes removed entirely. His monthly payment dropped even further.

What felt like a pipe dream became a dream home — with money left over every month.

Scenario 2 of 3

The Contingent Buyer

A volunteer "becomes" this person. Tell us about your dream move.

🙋‍♀️

Volunteer: You live in Herriman but your heart is set on Cottonwood Heights. Your agent just burst your bubble. Act it out.

👤 You Are...
You own a home in Herriman and want to move to Cottonwood Heights — a more coveted area with way hotter competition.

You came to this meeting thinking: "I'll never get an offer accepted."
❌ Your "Impossible" Problem
"My agent told me: 'If your offer is contingent on selling your Herriman house, they won't even look at it.' I have to sell before I can buy. But I can't buy without selling first. It's a chicken-and-egg problem in the most competitive market around. I'm stuck."
🎤

Tiffani takes over now.
She'll ask questions about your finances, your timeline, and find the path no one else saw.

💬 Group Question
Is this actually impossible? What's the workaround?
The Reveal

The Contingent Buyer — Solved

✅ What Tiffani Found
Her debt-to-income ratio on a conventional loan was too tight — she'd need to sell first.

But on an FHA loan, the DTI rules are more flexible. Tiffani realized: she could qualify carrying BOTH mortgage payments at once. That meant buying Cottonwood Heights without a sale contingency.
🎁 The Bonus Win
She closed on the new house, had 2 full weeks to move, then sold the Herriman home. No bridge loan. No double move. No storage unit.

Her agent's "impossible" became a seamless transition from one home to the next.
🦸‍♀️ Mortgage Superhero Strikes Again
Scenario 3 of 3

The Cash-Strapped Investor

A volunteer "becomes" this person. You've got assets but no cash. Make us feel it.

🙋‍♂️

Volunteer: You're a self-employed investor with a paid-off property and a can't-miss deal. But there's a $100,000 problem.

👤 You Are...
You found a $400,000 investment property that's fairly priced with huge potential. You need 25% down — about $100,000.

You're self-employed, so your income doesn't fit in a W-2 box. You came thinking: "I have assets, but I can't access them. This deal is dead."
❌ Your "Impossible" Problem
"I own a property free and clear, but I need that equity to buy this new one. And I'm self-employed — no traditional lender counts my income. I don't have $100K liquid. This deal dies unless I find a way to pull money out without selling."
🎤

Tiffani takes over now.
She'll look at your assets, your properties, and structure a loan where your property qualifies itself.

💬 Group Question
Is this actually impossible? How do you buy real estate with no cash and no traditional income?
The Reveal

The Cash-Strapped Investor — Solved

✅ What Tiffani Found
He owned an investment property free and clear. Tiffani structured a cash-out DSCR loan on that property.

DSCR = Debt Service Coverage Ratio. Based on the property's future rental income, not his personal income. Perfect for self-employed investors.
🎁 The Bonus Win
He pulled $100K from the paid-off property as his down payment. The new property had two entrances — rented to two families for ~$2,500 total, covering the new mortgage.

Zero personal income used. Zero cash out of pocket. One smart loan structure. His deal lived.
🦸‍♀️ Mortgage Superhero Strikes Again
