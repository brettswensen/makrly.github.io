FAQ

Questions?
We've got answers.

Everything you need to know about working with Liberty Roofing. Can't find what you're looking for? Call us.

How can I tell if my roof needs repair?
Look for missing or curling shingles, granules in gutters, water stains on ceilings, or daylight visible through the roof boards. If your roof is 20+ years old, it's time for an inspection — even if you don't see obvious damage.
How long does a roof replacement take?
Most residential reroofs take 1–2 days. Larger homes or complex rooflines may take 3–4 days. We work efficiently while never cutting corners on quality.
Do you work with insurance?
Absolutely. We document storm damage with photos and detailed reports, meet adjusters on-site, and handle the paperwork. Our goal is getting your claim approved without you having to chase anyone.
What's your warranty?
We offer a 25-year workmanship warranty on every full reroof — one of the strongest in Utah. Manufacturer warranties on materials range from 30 years to lifetime, depending on the shingle you choose.
Do I need to be home during the work?
Nope. As long as we have access to your roof and a way to reach you by phone, you can go about your day. We'll send photo updates and call when we're done for final walkthrough.
